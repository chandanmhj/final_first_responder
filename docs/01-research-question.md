# Research question (draft)

Status: **Proposed**.

Working paper title: *RAG for AI First Response*.

## Direction
The RAG system is the core of the paper. Every claim must be backed by measurements from the evaluation notebooks.

## Draft research question
Does a scenario-aware, step-structured retrieval pipeline with abstention give more accurate and safer emergency guidance than naive RAG,
and at what latency cost? The scenario engine (modules, roles, conflict rules) gives a natural structure for the scenario-aware part.

## Additional angles the project allows
- Role-conditioned answers (bystander, self, responder) and their safety.
- Calibrated confidence for image-based venomous-animal identification.

## Hypotheses and contributions
To be written after Phase 1 and the first experiments.

## Honest scope
The defensible claim is the best quality-versus-efficiency trade-off for this domain, shown by ablations against strong baselines.
