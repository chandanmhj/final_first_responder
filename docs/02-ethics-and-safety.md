# Ethics and safety

Status: rules marked **Proposed** await your confirmation.

## Intended use
Decision support for emergencies. It does not replace emergency services or a doctor.

## Rules
1. A visible "call 108" action is always available in chat. (Proposed)
2. Every medical step carries its source. (Proposed)
3. If retrieval is weak or the question is out of scope, the app says so instead of guessing. (Proposed)
4. **Animal bites and stings: the app states its confidence explicitly and always advises a hospital visit.** (Decided, item 19 in decisions)
   Because a photo cannot reliably rule out a venomous species, the confidence value must come from a measured, calibrated method,
   not from the model's own wording, and must be evaluated (notebook 05).
5. Never claim to diagnose. (Proposed)
6. Step content marked `extension` is not shown to users until a BCLS-trained or medical professional has reviewed it. (Proposed)
7. Medication steps are shown to trained first responders only. (Proposed, see gaps item 22)
8. **Source and permission:** handbook content is used only with the owner's permission and is attributed to its real source. (see docs/data/source-and-permissions.md)
9. Chat history and photos are stored permanently (Decided). A privacy policy and a delete-my-data option are needed. (Proposed)
10. Secrets never appear in the frontend except the public anon key and the hCaptcha site key.
