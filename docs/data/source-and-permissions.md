# Guideline source and permission

Status: **Open.** Needs your decision (see OPEN_QUESTIONS.md, question 1).

## The source you provided
*Basic Emergency Care & Life Support (BCLS) Provider Handbook, Second Edition, January 2025*, published by the
RGUHS-JeevaRaksha Trust (Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences and Swami Vivekananda Youth Movement).

The cover says: *"Not for publication, duplication, quotation or citation without prior permission of
RGUHS-JeevaRaksha Trust."*

## Why this matters for this project
- The app would reproduce and paraphrase the handbook's content, and the research paper would describe and evaluate it.
  Both fall under "publication, duplication, quotation or citation".
- The handbook is based on its own references (for example AHA 2020 guidelines, WHO, ESC). Content derived from the
  handbook cannot honestly be labelled as coming from WHO or the Red Cross. In a paper, wrong attribution counts
  as research misconduct, and in an app it misleads users about who stands behind medical advice.
- So the app and the data files state the real source, and each step carries a handbook page number for traceability.

## Options
1. **Ask the Trust for written permission** (contact printed in the handbook: team@jeevaraksha.org) to use the handbook
   as the knowledge base of a student project and to publish results about it. This keeps your content, and a
   permission letter strengthens the paper. Mention the university project and the non-commercial use.
2. **Rebuild the knowledge base from openly licensed sources** (for example WHO publications and Red Cross/IFRC first aid
   guidance). I have not checked their licences. Each document's terms must be checked before use, and the content would
   differ from the handbook.
3. **Keep the handbook for private development only** and do not publish the app or the paper until permission or a replacement is in place.

Until you decide, the repository keeps handbook-based steps for development only. Nothing here should be published,
deployed publicly or submitted to a journal before this is settled.
